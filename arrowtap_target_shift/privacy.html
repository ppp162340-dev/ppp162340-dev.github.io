Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 7, 2026
This Privacy Policy elaborates our complete standards for data gathering, processing, storage and authorized disclosure when you use the mobile game ArrowTap: Target Shift (referred to as “the Application”). Installing, launching, or continuously interacting with this Application signifies that you have fully read, understood, and voluntarily accepted every clause laid out in this policy. We reserve the right to revise the policy content flexibly to adapt to service upgrades, platform compliance rules and updated local data protection laws, and we recommend users check the privacy page within App Settings frequently to stay informed of revisions.

1. Categories of Data We Gather

We only collect data that is essential to guarantee stable game operation, polish target tapping puzzle gameplay and deliver seamless user experience. No sensitive personal data will be collected without your clear active consent. All collected data is divided into five independent categories as follows:

  1.                 Login & Personal Account Information
    If you authorize sign-in via supported third-party platforms such as Google Account, Apple ID, or Facebook, we will receive your bound email address, public display name and profile avatar image. This data is exclusively used to build your unique game account, finish identity verification, and synchronize target challenge archives across multiple mobile devices. We will never access extra private details irrelevant to account registration.
  1.                 Device and Network Technical Metadata
    The Application automatically collects non-traceable device identifiers including GAID for Android and IDFA for iOS, public IP address, device model, operating system build version, screen pixel resolution, default system language, time zone configuration, and active network mode (Wi-Fi or cellular data). This metadata helps us adjust target layout sizes for diverse screen hardware and fix intermittent network disconnection issues during target tapping gameplay.
  1.                 Target Challenge Game Interaction Records
    We record anonymous in-game behavioral metrics such as single-target tap response frequency, stage clearance speed, level restart times, hint tool utilization count, exclusive skin activation records, daily play session length, and interactions with reward-bearing ads. All such records are processed in aggregated anonymous form to adjust stage difficulty curves, launch new target challenge packs, and optimize core game operation logic.
  1.                 Fault & Performance Diagnostic Logs
    When the Application encounters crashes, frame freezes, loading failures or abnormal lag during target tapping, the system automatically generates diagnostic logs containing error codes, real-time memory occupancy and the exact target stage where the fault occurred. These logs are only deployed to locate technical defects and push subsequent version patches, without embedding any identifiable user markers.
  1.                 Optional Installed App Catalogue Data (User Consent Mandatory)
    Our embedded advertising SDK may pop up a permission request to access the list of other applications installed on your device at the first startup. You possess full authority to reject this request, and refusal will not restrict access to any core target-shifting puzzle functions. The development team of ArrowTap: Target Shift cannot view or store this catalogue data; all relevant processing is independently undertaken by the advertising SDK provider under its separate privacy rules.

2. Legal and Authorized Purposes of Data Utilization

All collected data shall only be processed for service-related legitimate purposes listed below, and we will never repurpose your data for irrelevant commercial marketing without your separate explicit written approval:

•                      Maintain full core gameplay functionality: synchronize target tapping progress, finished challenge stages, unlocked arrow skins and accumulated in-game rewards across all your logged-in devices;

•                      Optimize game frame smoothness, tap touch sensitivity and layout compatibility for all mainstream Android and iOS mobile hardware;

•                      Deliver moderate non-intrusive in-app advertisements and ad-watch reward missions by leveraging device advertising identifiers;

•                      Receive, classify and respond to your gameplay feedback, difficulty adjustment suggestions and technical fault reports submitted through the built-in support channel;

•                      Send in-app push notifications regarding new target stage updates, limited-time arrow skin events and official privacy policy revision announcements;

•                      Identify fake registered accounts, repeated reward claim fraud and automated tap script abuse to sustain a fair gameplay environment for all users.

3. Limited Scenarios Permitting Data Disclosure

We strictly commit to never selling, leasing or trading your personally identifiable information to external merchants for mass commercial promotion. Data disclosure is only allowed under the following legally compliant, restricted circumstances:

•                      We may share your device advertising identifier with certified cooperative ad partners to complete ad rendering, exposure statistics and reward task matching. All collaborating ad vendors sign binding confidentiality contracts and are prohibited from reusing this identifier for unrelated commercial projects;

•                      Aggregated, fully anonymized statistical data (without any user traceable tags) can be shared with third-party data analysis institutions for long-term game iteration and user experience optimization;

•                      Authorized service suppliers including cloud storage operators and crash log analysis tools can access limited operational data required to complete their assigned duties, and they must comply with identical privacy protection standards specified within this policy;

•                      We may disclose the minimum necessary scope of your data upon receiving formal court summons, regulatory investigation orders or mandatory legal requests issued by competent official authorities.

4. Multi-Layer Data Security Protection System

We implement comprehensive technical and administrative protective measures to block unauthorized access, data tampering, information leakage and permanent loss of your data:

•                      All data transmitted between your mobile terminal and our backend server is encrypted via TLS transmission protocol; all stored account archives are protected by high-strength static encryption technology;

•                      Tiered internal access permission control is enforced: only maintenance and customer support staff with necessary job requirements can view partial user data, and all relevant personnel are bound by signed non-disclosure agreements;

•                      Regular vulnerability scanning, server security audits and Application security patch updates are scheduled to defend against emerging network security risks.

Kindly note that no electronic storage or online data transmission technology can achieve 100% absolute data security. We will adopt all commercially viable protective countermeasures to safeguard your privacy, yet complete data security cannot be fully guaranteed under all network environments.

5. Unified Data Retention Specifications

We only preserve your data within the shortest valid period required to fulfill collection objectives or satisfy legal archiving obligations:

•                      Your login account information, saved target challenge stages and arrow skin inventory will be retained while your account remains active; once your deletion application passes verification, all these identifiable data will be erased from servers instantly;

•                      Anonymous game behavior logs and fault diagnostic records will be stored for a maximum of 9 months, after which the data will be automatically anonymized or completely cleared;

•                      Partial non-identifiable anti-fraud markers may be reserved for a short limited period to prevent repeated reward abuse, and will be permanently deleted once anti-fraud monitoring work concludes;

•                      Data that needs to be archived to comply with tax, accounting and local regulatory requirements will be retained until the statutory retention cycle expires.
