Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use the mobile application Spherey Run: Move & Clear (the “Service”). By downloading, installing, or using the Service, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. We only use your information to provide, maintain, and improve the Service, and we will not share your data except as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to support gameplay, stability, and user experience:

•                      Account Information: When you log in using Apple ID, Google, or Facebook, we receive your email address, display name, and profile picture. This is used to create your account, verify your identity, and sync game progress across devices.

•                      Advertising ID (IDFA/GAID): We collect your device’s advertising identifier to prevent fraud, improve ad quality, and deliver relevant in-game advertisements.

•                      IP Address: Used to determine general region, protect server security, and prevent unauthorized access.

•                      Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, system language, time zone, session timestamps, and network status. Used to optimize performance and compatibility.

•                      Gameplay & Log Data: Records of in-game actions (movement, clearing, level completion, score, power-up usage), error logs, and crash reports. Used to fix bugs, balance difficulty, and improve the game.

•                      Installed Apps (with consent): Our ad SDK may request installed app information on first launch. You may decline; this does not affect core gameplay. We do not access or store this data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information only for the following purposes:

•                      Provide and maintain the core gameplay experience, including progress saving and leaderboards (if available).

•                      Optimize game performance, responsiveness, and visual display across different devices.

•                      Deliver non-intrusive, personalized advertisements and optional reward tasks.

•                      Respond to your customer support inquiries and resolve technical issues.

•                      Notify you of important policy or game updates.

•                      Detect cheating, fake accounts, and abuse of in-game systems.

3. How We Disclose Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in limited situations:

•                      Trusted ad partners: To deliver advertisements using your IDFA/GAID.

•                      Authorized service providers: Cloud hosting, analytics, and crash reporting (bound by confidentiality agreements).

•                      Legal requirements: When required by law, court order, or government request.

•                      Business transfers: In a merger, acquisition, or asset sale; the new party will follow this Privacy Policy.

4. Data Security

We protect your information using industry-standard security measures:

•                      TLS 1.3 encryption for data transmission between your device and our servers.

•                      AES‑256 encryption for stored personal data.

•                      Strict access control for internal staff and third parties.

•                      Regular security audits and system updates.

While we maintain strong protection, no internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary:

•                      Account and game progress data: kept while your account is active.

•                      After deletion request: personal data is deleted immediately.

•                      Non‑identifiable fraud‑prevention data: retained up to 6 months.

•                      Anonymized analytics: retained for game improvement.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You may request permanent deletion of your account and data at any time:

  1.                 In‑app: Go to Settings → Privacy → Delete Account.
  1.                 By email: Send a request to ppp162340@gmail.com with the subject line: Spherey Run Account Deletion.

We will process deletion within 14 business days. Once deleted, progress, scores, and rewards cannot be recovered.

7. Log Data

When the app crashes or errors occur, we collect Log Data via third‑party tools. This includes IP address, device model, OS version, error state, time, and technical details. Used only to diagnose and fix problems. No personal identity is included.

8. Cookies & Similar Technologies

With your permission, we may access contacts for fraud prevention and friend invites. If you revoke permission, stored contact data is deleted within 3 business days.
